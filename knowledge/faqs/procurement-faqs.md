# Procurement and Accounts Payable FAQs

Source: https://www.babson.edu/business-and-financial-services/procurement/faqs/
Last reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Workday

**How do I get a new supplier added to Workday?**
Submit a request through the Workday Strategic Sourcing portal. See payee-onboarding.md.

**How do I, as a payee or supplier, onboard to Babson?**
Submit the Payee Onboarding Form through the Workday Strategic Sourcing portal. See payee-onboarding.md.

**When should I submit my request for goods or services in Workday?**
After the supplier is confirmed in Workday by Accounts Payable, and before goods or services are delivered or rendered.

**When do I need a PO number?**
PO numbers are required for all requests over $10,000. Procurement creates them from supplier contracts or requisitions submitted into Workday. Some requests over $10,000 may not need a PO, so follow up with Procurement.

**When should I use a Requisition for Goods?**
When requesting goods over $10,000.

**When should I use a Requisition for Services?**
When procuring services and an agreement isn't required, mostly orders under $10,000 each year. Exceptions may apply.

**When should I use a Supplier Contract for Services?**
When procuring services and an agreement is required, mostly orders over $10,000 each year. Exceptions may apply.

**Can I use my P-Card to pay for services, such as independent contractors, consultants, and awards?**
No. It should only be used to pay for goods under $10,000.

**How do I submit an invoice for payment?**
Submit a "Supplier Invoice Request" in Workday. Attach the invoice and note the PO number if it's over $10,000. If an invoice is under $10,000 but ties to a contract over $10,000, include the PO number on all invoice requests.

## Contracts

**When does a contract need Legal review?**
When it's a non-college template or an altered college template. Submit through the Contract Submittal Form: https://babson.tfaforms.net/102 An unaltered college template doesn't need legal review. It can be signed and submitted through Workday for business review.

**How long does Procurement's business review take?**
Two days or less.

**How long does Legal review take?**
Two weeks or less.

**How long does an unaltered contract take to be reviewed in Workday?**
24-48 hours.

**What type of contract should I use?**
See the Contract Templates page: https://www.babson.edu/business-and-financial-services/procurement/contract-templates/

**When should contracts be signed?**
Unaltered contracts can be signed by both parties with no Legal review. Vendor contracts or altered Babson templates should only be signed after Legal review.

## Insurance

**What is a Certificate of Insurance (COI)?**
A one-page document summarizing proof of a supplier's business insurance coverage. Required coverages are in insurance-and-risk.md.

**When is a COI required?**
Any time two contracting parties sign an agreement to work together, especially if the work is on campus.
