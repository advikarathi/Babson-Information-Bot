## Getting started

**What changed?** Beginning October 13, 2026, Amazon purchases for Babson business are started in Workday, which connects you to Amazon Business. Your cart returns to Workday for approval before the order is placed. This replaces using a P-Card, creating an expense report, and uploading receipts.

**Where do I start?** In Workday, through the Sidebar under Organization > Requisition, or the Create Requisition task.

**Do I need a separate login?** No. But if your Babson email is tied to a personal Amazon account, you may need a short setup step. See the registration job aid.

**Can I buy directly on Amazon outside Workday, or with a personal account?** No. Purchases through personal Amazon accounts can't use a P-Card and won't be reimbursed. The Punchout ensures business pricing and sales tax exemption.

## Shopping

**Does "Submit Order for Approval" place my order?** No. It sends your cart to Workday so you can complete and submit the requisition. The order isn't placed until approvals are complete.

**Are prices guaranteed?** No. Approvals should be completed within 14 calendar days of the cart transfer. Outside that window, pricing or inventory may change.

**My session timed out.** The cart may not transfer. Return to the requisition and restart the Punchout session.

**Can I buy gift cards?** No. For an approved business need, contact Procurement and follow the existing gift card process.

## Approvals

**Who approves?** The same approval structure as expense reports, but approval comes before the order is placed.

**What's a restricted item?** It can still be bought, but may need additional Procurement approval. A blocked item can't be added to the cart at all.

**What happens after final approval?** Workday creates a purchase order and sends it to Amazon. You get a confirmation email with the Amazon order number, PO number, items, shipping, and estimated delivery date.

## Shipping

**Where do orders ship?** To Babson campus locations selected in Workday.

**Can I ship to my home?** Only with an approved Ship-to-Home Exception, limited to fully remote employees who don't regularly report to campus, or an approved one-time need for an eligible large home-office item. Contact Procurement.

## Requisitions and tracking

**Do I attach receipts?** No, not for a standard Punchout purchase. You also don't need to approve the Amazon invoice afterward.

**What about worktags?** Complete Gift, Grant, or Project only when the purchase is tied to that funding source.

**Can I change the cart in Workday?** You can remove items before submitting, or choose Continue Shopping to add more.

**How do I check status?** Search My Requisitions in Workday and run the report with a date range. Open a requisition and select Process History to see approval steps. Track shipments under Your Orders in Amazon Business.

**Why might an order be halted or cancelled?** Approvals not completed within the 14-day cart-lock period, multiple Punchout sessions for one order, certain cart changes in Workday after transfer, or certain shipping address or ZIP code changes. You may need to submit a new order.

## Returns and help

**How do I return or report a damaged item?** Access Amazon Business through Requisitions or My Requisitions in Workday, select Connect to Supplier Website, then go to Your Orders. Credit is applied as a negative invoice. You don't need a new requisition.

**Who do I contact?** Procurement (procurement@babson.edu) for process, policy, restricted items, or Workday questions. Amazon Customer Support for shipment, delivery, or charge issues.
