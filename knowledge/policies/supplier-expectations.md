## Supplier Expectations

Babson College is committed to promoting lawful, sustainable, and socially responsible procurement practices. Suppliers acknowledge their commitment to these expectations by doing business with Babson College.

Compliance with laws and regulations:
- Comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws and regulations.
- Uphold ethical business practices and avoid illegal or unethical activities.

Labor and human rights:
- Treat employees with dignity and respect.
- Prohibit child labor, forced labor, and any form of discrimination.
- Provide fair wages, safe working conditions, and reasonable working hours.

Environmental responsibility:
- Minimize adverse environmental impacts.
- Promote sustainable practices, waste reduction, and energy efficiency.
- Comply with all applicable environmental laws and regulations.

Anti-corruption and bribery:
- Do not engage in bribery, corruption, or unlawful practices.
- Maintain transparency and integrity in all business dealings.

Conflict of interest:
- Have a process that requires prompt disclosure and appropriate management of potential and actual conflicts of interest.

Privacy and data protection:
- Protect sensitive information and respect applicable privacy rights.
- Comply with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

Continuous improvement:
- Improve practices continuously.
- Collaborate with Babson to enhance sustainability and social responsibility practices affecting Babson.

Accountability:
- Where expectations are not met, Babson will assess compliance and take appropriate actions.

## Code of Ethics

Babson adheres to these principles, based in part on the Code of Ethics of the National Association of Educational Buyers, New England:

- Give first consideration to the objectives and policies of Babson College.
- Strive to obtain the maximum value for each dollar of expenditure.
- Decline personal gifts or gratuities.
- Grant competitive and qualified suppliers equal consideration insofar as state or federal statute and institutional policy permit.
- Conduct business with potential and current suppliers in an atmosphere devoid of intentional misrepresentation.
- Demand honesty in sales representation, whether verbal, written, in an advertisement, or in a product sample.
- Receive consent of the originator of proprietary ideas and designs before using them for competitive purchasing purposes.
- Accord a prompt and courteous reception, as conditions permit, to all who call on legitimate business missions.
- Cooperate with trade, industrial, and professional associations and governmental and private agencies to promote sound business methods.
- Foster fair, ethical, and legal trade practices.
- Manage business in a sustainable and responsible way and seek suppliers who share that commitment.

Each vendor is responsible for ensuring its representatives understand and comply with this Code of Ethics.
