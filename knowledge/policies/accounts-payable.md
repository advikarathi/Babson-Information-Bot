## About the office

The Accounts Payable Office provides financial management and customer service to suppliers and campus partners and ensures timely and accurate payments for goods and services. It works with Procurement, Insurance, and Finance.

## Who to contact

See contacts.md. For P-Card questions, email pcard@babson.edu. The website does not publish a general Accounts Payable email address or phone number.

## Submitting an invoice for payment

- Submit a "Supplier Invoice Request" in Workday. Attach a copy of the invoice and note the PO number if the invoice is over $10,000.
- If an invoice is under $10,000 but ties back to a contract exceeding $10,000, include the PO number on all invoice requests.

Invoice address: Babson College, Accounts Payable Department, P.O. Box 57310, Babson Park, MA 02457-0310.

## Payment terms

If procurement procedures are followed, Accounts Payable pays within Babson's payment terms, 2/10, net/30.

Accounts Payable questions all bills that cannot be associated with an authorized purchase order, and any expense reports that include equipment, software, or other goods and services that would normally require a purchase order.

Accounts Payable must have a fully executed copy of an agreement on file from Procurement before making payment to a counterparty.

## Adding a new supplier or payee

A new payee must complete onboarding, including payment and tax verification, before payment can be processed. See payee-onboarding.md.

## Expense reimbursements

Accounts Payable provides reimbursement within two weeks when a travel expense report is submitted on time with proper approval and required documentation. File it no later than three weeks after completing a trip.
