## Mission

The office fosters partnerships that promote economic growth and create social impact. It empowers small and underserved businesses with support from local communities while advocating for ethical business practices.

## The program

- Babson is committed to expanding the breadth of suppliers it utilizes and partners with across campus.
- It actively seeks to attract qualified small, disadvantaged, and underserved business enterprises.
- It commits to increasing opportunity annually for small, underserved, veteran-owned (including retired), and socially and economically disadvantaged business enterprises.
- It educates and supports small and disadvantaged suppliers within the community.

## Why it matters

Stronger relationships through trust and open communication; improved supplier performance on quality and delivery; innovation from suppliers who share ideas; early identification of supply chain risks; and support for small and underserved businesses, which also expands the supplier base.

## Small and Underserved Supplier Program application

Apply online: https://www.babson.edu/business-and-financial-services/office-of-supplier-and-community-engagement/small-and-underserved-supplier-program-application/

To be recognized through the program, companies must be certified through an official certification agency. The application asks for company and contact information, type of business (manufacturer, distributor, service, other), product or service categories, NAICS codes, Group Purchasing Organization membership, percentage of sales within education, designations, and an uploaded certification.

Designations on the application: SBA 8(a); Self-Certified Small Disadvantaged Business (SC-SDB); Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE); Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB); Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (ED-WOSB); HUBZone; Veteran Owned Business (VET); Service Disabled Veteran (SDVET); Disabled; AbilityOne Program; Small Business Enterprise (SBE); Airport Concession Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (ACDBE).

Most ownership-based designations require at least 51% ownership by the qualifying individuals. Exact definitions are on the agencies page below.
