Applies to employees and students driving motor vehicles, including golf carts and utility vehicles, on College business.

## Who must be authorized

Drivers of vehicles owned, leased, or rented in the College's name; employees required to drive for their job; and employees driving personal vehicles on the College's behalf. Applicants need a valid U.S. driver's license held for at least one year.

## How to get authorized

1. In Workday, click Requests, then Create Request, type "driver authorization", and submit the application.
2. Driver history review: if your application is acceptable, you get an email from CSI to provide details for your motor vehicle record. Check junk mail. The review takes about 24-72 hours, is confidential, and doesn't affect your credit score.
3. Complete driver safety courses: defensive driver and van safety for vehicles other than golf carts or gators, or golf cart safety for golf carts or gators.
4. Contact Public Safety to schedule a vehicle orientation.

The policy says authorized drivers are notified for an update "on a tri-annual basis". It doesn't define that term, so quote those words and don't convert them into a specific number of months or years. Not responding removes you from the list. Report any license expiration, suspension, or revocation to Public Safety immediately.

## Reserving College vehicles

- Fleet vehicles are for official College business only. No personal use.
- Reserve online through EMS. Questions: Office of Student Engagement, 781-239-4500.
- The 6-seat golf cart is reserved by a work order to Facilities Management & Planning through the Babson Portal.
- Inspect before and after use and report damage to Public Safety. Don't use vehicles with obvious safety problems. List passengers on the Pre-Trip Inspection Form.
- Return clean with a full tank. Gas is available at Facilities 24/7. If you can't return on time, call Public Safety at 781-239-5555.

## Rental vehicles

Rent in the College's name with a P-Card and follow the Travel Guidelines. See business-travel.md.

Inspect and photograph the vehicle at pickup and return. Report damage to the rental agency, your department manager, and Risk Management (781-239-5688) immediately upon return to campus, no later than 1 week after returning the vehicle.

## Personal vehicles

Keep them maintained, registered, and inspected, with liability insurance meeting Massachusetts minimums. Your own insurance is primary, and the College doesn't cover physical damage to your vehicle.

## Accidents

1. Move away from the road, exiting away from traffic if possible. If anyone is hurt, call police and emergency medical help immediately. Don't move an injured person unless conditions are threatening.
2. Acknowledge only facts. Don't say you or the College is responsible, and avoid words like "fault" and "blame".
3. Notify local police after any accident, however minor, and get the report details.
4. Notify Public Safety (781-239-5555) and Risk Management (781-239-5688). On campus, notify Public Safety immediately.
5. For a rental, notify the rental agency. For a personal vehicle, notify your insurer. If an employee is injured, contact Human Resources about workers' compensation.

Departments pay for fleet damage caused by their driver, up to the deductible, currently $1,000, within 30 days. If the accident isn't reported to Public Safety within 24 hours, the department may be responsible for the entire loss.

## Breakdowns and repairs

If a fleet vehicle breaks down off campus, call Public Safety at 781-239-5555. Don't have repairs made without their authorization. The College won't reimburse unauthorized repairs.

## Rules of use

- Seat belts are required for everyone.
- No alcohol or drugs, including impairing medications, before or while driving. Alcohol and drugs may not be transported in College vehicles.
- Only authorized drivers may drive, and each must be on the trip sheet.
- Drivers and their departments are responsible for tickets and fines.
- When transporting students more than 180 miles or three hours one way, a second authorized driver is required. No driver may go beyond that without a two-hour rest.
- Outsource transportation for students traveling over 600 miles one way, or for trips extending past 2:00 AM, unless the Risk Manager or Public Safety Director approves an exception.
- No items on the roof, moving equipment, towing, or hazardous cargo, with exceptions for Facilities Management and in some cases ITSD.
- Children under 12 may ride only under limited conditions. Check the Vehicle Use page.
- Golf carts and utility vehicles may not be driven on public roads, including Forest Street, Wellesley Avenue, and Great Plain Avenue, or after dark without head and tail lamps. Pedestrians have the right of way, and operators must be employees aged 18 or over with a valid license.
