## Certificates of insurance (COI)

**From a supplier to Babson:** a COI is a one-page summary of a supplier's insurance coverage. It should be required any time two parties sign an agreement to work together, especially for work on campus. To request a waiver, note it in the Memo section of the contract submission and Procurement will advise.

| Standard coverage | Amount |
|---|---|
| Commercial General Liability | $1,000,000 each occurrence, $2,000,000 aggregate |
| Comprehensive Automobile (non-owned and hired) | $1,000,000 |
| Workers Compensation | $500,000 |

Babson should be listed as additional insured whenever possible: "Babson College, The Trustees of Babson College, its officers, employees, and agents are included as additional insured where required by written contract regarding General and Auto Liability, Waiver of subrogation is applicable where required by written contract regarding General Liability, Auto Liability, and Workers Compensation."

**From Babson to someone else:** complete the Request for Certificate of Insurance form and send it to Risk Management. Most requests take 48 hours but some take several weeks. Form: https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/risk-management/request-for-certificate-of-insurance.pdf

## Reporting incidents and claims

| What happened | Who to contact |
|---|---|
| Injury to a student in a Babson program, damage to Babson property, or injury or damage caused by Babson to a third party | Risk Management, 781-239-5688 |
| Injury or illness of an employee on the job | Jennifer Forbes, Director of Benefits, Human Resources, jforbes2@babson.edu, 781-239-5224, Nichols Hall Room 137 |

**In an emergency, call 911.** Public Safety is 781-239-5555 (ext. 5555).

**Liability incident:** contact Public Safety, within 24 hours if off campus. Don't make any statements about liability or payment of bills. Public Safety investigates and informs Risk Management, which notifies insurers. Guests and vendors can contact Public Safety or Risk Management.

**Property damage or theft:** notify Public Safety, which reports to Facilities and Risk Management. Send clean-up and repair cost documentation to Risk Management.

**Workers' Compensation:** employees, including student employees, must immediately tell their supervisor. The supervisor reports to Human Resources and completes a First Report of Injury form.

**Vehicle accidents:** see driver-and-vehicle-use.md.

## Insurance for organizations using Babson facilities

Organizations licensing Babson facilities, including athletic facilities, Glavin Chapel, Sorensen Theater, residence halls, classrooms, the skating rink, and conference rooms, must have insurance that names Babson as additional insured. Those without coverage can buy:

- **Events (TULIP) coverage** for a specific event. Not for youth camps. https://tulip.ajgrms.com/Content/Brochures/URMIA_TULIP_Special_Events_Brochure.pdf
- **URMIA Youth Camps Insurance** for youth camps. https://ucamps.rpsins.com/ If a camp's activity isn't automatically approved, contact Carolyn Budelman, Carolyn_Budelman@RPSins.com, 1-973-921-8368.

## Liability releases (waivers)

A release is required if an activity is voluntary (not required by curriculum or job) and involves risk of injury, death, or loss of personal property, or involves international travel.

- Make it specific to the event dates, description, and risks.
- Tell participants a release is required when announcing the event.
- Get it signed at least 3 days before the event, or 14 days for international travel.
- Use Adobe Sign for signers with a Babson.edu email. Others must return a paper release with an original signature. Email and fax copies aren't acceptable.
- Records are kept at least 7 years.

Templates: Adobe Sign version https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/risk-management/Standard_Liability_Release-new.pdf and live signature version https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/business-and-financial-services/Standard_Liability_Release_With_Witness.pdf

## Assessing event risk

The Risk Management Guide for Activity/Event Organizers rates an event by type, then adds risk modifiers such as alcohol, College transportation, off-campus location, large crowds, uninsured third parties, animals, minors, or unlicensed food vendors.

- **Low:** proceed with your manager's or director's approval.
- **Moderate:** submit to your department director, who decides if the Risk Manager should review. May need a waiver.
- **High:** needs prior authorization from the Risk Manager and General Counsel, and will require a waiver as determined by the Risk Manager.

Guide (February 2011): https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/risk-management/risk-management-guide.pdf
