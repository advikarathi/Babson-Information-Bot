## Printers and copiers

Babson prioritizes shared Canon multifunction devices (MFDs) and aims to reduce local personal devices. MFDs default to double-sided, black and white, and sleep mode. Settings can be changed per job.

P-Cards can't be used for printers, scanners, copiers, or fax machines. Order these through Babson IT.

### Requesting a personal printer

A local personal device needs formal approval from the area's Vice President or Budget & Operating Committee member, with the Budget and Procurement Services member. The department pays for it and its supplies. Criteria include:
- The employee is at VP level or above
- The nearest MFD or networked LaserJet is over 100 feet away, or there is none in the building or on the floor
- Consistent printing of at least 5 or 10 jobs a day of highly sensitive material that secure print release can't handle
- Time-sensitive small color jobs with no nearby color MFD
- Regular special printing needs defined by the job
- Walking to the MFD would be a substantial hardship

## Print jobs

Use the Digital Store Front to request and track jobs from the Canon Copy Center: https://babsoncollege.myprintdesk.net/DSF/SmartStore.aspx#!/Storefront

In areas without high-production devices, send jobs over 100 pages to the Copy Center. Departments are charged for binders and posters. The Copy Center may stop a job that could violate copyright until clearance is shown.

## Scanning

Scan to email is the accepted method, and scanning is encouraged over faxing. Departments scanning Personal Information or confidential data need prior Data Security Committee approval. Copyrighted material needs written permission from the owner.

## Green purchasing

Babson strives to balance environmental and fiscal responsibility, promoting products with the highest recycled content available that meet use and performance standards. It prefers materials that are:
- Durable, not single use or disposable
- Non-toxic or minimally toxic, preferably biodegradable
- Highly energy-efficient
- Recyclable or safely disposable
- Made from sustainably obtained raw materials and manufactured in an environmentally sound way
- Causing minimal or no damage during use or maintenance
- Shipped with minimal, preferably recycled or recyclable packaging

Copy paper preference is at least 30 percent recycled content. When appropriate, bid specifications include: "Babson is committed to buying products with recycled content or environmentally sustainable alternatives. Please offer any alternatives that you feel are available for this product and supply all relevant specific information about the product."
