## Choosing the right vehicle

Babson acquires goods and services through four types of contracts: P-Card, PO, College Template Agreement, and Non-Template Agreement.

- Goods: use a P-Card or PO for all goods other than unique or hazardous goods.
- Services: a P-Card or PO should not be used as the contract for services, except as stated on the Procurement website. Use a College template agreement.
- If a P-Card or PO can't be used, use a College template whenever possible.

The department seeking the contract currently determines which to use. Questions: procurement@babson.edu.

## Which template?

Templates are on the Contract Templates page, accessed with a Babson username and password: https://www.babson.edu/business-and-financial-services/procurement/contract-templates/

| Template | Use for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Professional Services Agreement | Buying knowledge or expertise, usually project related, where IP ownership is key or confidential information is shared | Consulting, coaching, web developers, marketing and advertising |
| Service Provider Agreement | Large labor-related services, not expertise | Catering, staging, painting, tent and table setup, paving, roofing, training, workshops, bus transportation |
| Short Form Service Provider Agreement | Work for hire up to $7,500 | Small construction renovations, painting |
| Speaker/Performer Agreement | Artistic performers and public speaking | Singers, dancers, speakers, readers, musicians |
| Guest Speaker Agreement | Small speaking engagements, not to exceed $1,500 | Course-related guest speakers |
| Photographer/Videographer Services Agreement | Those services | Photographers, videographers |
| Volunteer Status Confirmation, Confidentiality & Release Agreement | Services where the provider isn't paid | Help during Commencement |
| Hotel/Venue Addendum | Hotel, catering, or events where the Service Provider Agreement isn't used | Blocking hotel rooms, reserving a venue |

Also on that page: Employee/Independent Contractor Certification Form (https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/procurement/IC-Form-7.20.pdf) and the IT software and project request form (https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/35e5c20ad3814c63b54b2975e37ddf4f).

See contract-terms-review.md for tips on completing templates and common pitfalls.

## When Legal review is needed

- Non-college template, or an altered college template: legal approval is required. Submit for business and legal review through the Contract Submittal Form: https://babson.tfaforms.net/102
- Unaltered college template: legal review isn't required. It can be signed and submitted through Workday for business review.

A template is altered if the vendor attached its own terms and conditions or changed any terms.

## Review turnaround

| Review | Turnaround |
|---|---|
| Procurement business review | 2 days or less |
| Legal review | 2 weeks or less |
| Unaltered contract reviewed in Workday | 24-48 hours |

Legal review may include negotiation with the vendor, which can lengthen it. Seek help from Procurement as early as possible.

## Submitting a non-template contract

1. Complete the Contract Submittal Form: https://babson.tfaforms.net/102
2. Attach the Word version of the proposed agreement with all exhibits, plus any previous or existing agreement and any required Certificate of Insurance.
3. Send the form and attachments to contracts@babson.edu. If using a template, send a PDF of it.
4. Procurement does the business review and coordinates insurance and risk review with Risk Management.
5. The Office of the General Counsel does the legal review for non-template agreements.

## Signing

- Unaltered contracts can be signed by both parties with no Legal review.
- Vendor contracts or altered Babson templates should only be signed after Legal review.
- Contracts must be signed by all parties before any work begins.
- Only designated signatories authorized through the Audit Committee of the Board of Trustees may sign for the College. Budgetary or spending authority isn't contract signature authority.
- Contracts signed without appropriate review or authorized signatures are invalid.
- Send a copy of the fully executed agreement to Procurement. Accounts Payable needs it on file before payment.

The department sending the agreement is responsible for choosing the form of contract, describing its purpose, ensuring the business and payment terms are correct, noting whether it's an unaltered template, completing the submittal, and complying with other College policies.
