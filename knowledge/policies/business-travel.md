## Booking

Use the Campus Travel Portal or an authorized agency whenever possible, but use a lower-cost source if one is available. Book as soon as plans are final to get advance purchase discounts.

- Travel Portal: https://campustravel.com/university/babson-portal/
- Cleveland Circle Travel: 1034 Great Plain Ave, Needham, MA 02492. 781-449-6860, info@cctravel.com, www.cctravel.com
- Protravel International: One Appleton Street, 2nd Floor, Boston, MA 02116. 617-840-1631, Beth Goldberg, www.protravelinc.com

Agencies offer live 24/7 support and require a valid Babson expense account number and P-Card number. You don't have to use them if you find a lower fare elsewhere, have an emergency itinerary change, a conference host arranged discounts they can't match, a business client asks you to use its agency, or you're part of a group buying tickets through another agency.

It is strongly recommended to use a Babson P-Card for all business travel. Personal credit cards are highly discouraged. Obtain prior authorization for travel and entertainment expenses. See p-card.md for spending limits.

## Domestic air (continental U.S. and Puerto Rico)

Book on a P-Card and fly economy. Business class is allowed when:
- The traveler has certain physical conditions or disabilities, documented through the ADA accommodation process (employeeaccommodations@babson.edu)
- It costs no more than the lowest coach fare
- Coach is sold out and no alternate flights are available
- Traveling with clients, who may be matched on class of service

Document an explanation in Workday when using business class.

Upgrades at College expense aren't permitted unless the total ticket cost does not exceed the lowest posted airfare. Travelers may use their own frequent flyer miles or personally pay, but upgrades may not be charged to the P-Card. Babson does not reimburse tickets bought with frequent flyer miles. Changes after booking need approval from the appropriate Vice President, Division Director, or Division Chair.

## International air (all destinations outside the continental U.S. and Puerto Rico)

Notify the travel agencies at least three weeks before departure. Business class is allowed when:
- The traveler has certain physical conditions or disabilities, documented through the ADA accommodation process
- Coach is sold out and no alternate flights are available
- Total flying time exceeds seven hours, excluding layovers

First class isn't allowed, subject to exception by the Board of Trustees or the President. Document business class in Workday. The upgrade rules above apply.

Travel to an Elevated Risk Destination, including a State Department Level 3 or 4 advisory, needs prior written authorization under the Policy on International Travel and the Use of Electronic Devices Abroad.

Convert foreign expenses to U.S. dollars, with the rate and source on each receipt. P-Card charges are converted by the card provider.

## International SOS (ISOS)

ISOS provides travel advice and emergency medical and security services to students and employees traveling on College business or programs, during the approved travel plus a total of seven days outside it. It is not health insurance and does not cover the cost of medical care.

- Travelers abroad on College business or programs are required to download the ISOS app.
- Plan on the Babson/ISOS site: https://www.internationalsos.com/MasterPortal/default.aspx?CustNo1=Enter+membership+number&membnum=11BSGC000046
- Email your itinerary to BabsonCollegeTravel@itinerary.internationalsos.com
- Tell JP Morgan about international P-Card use. See p-card.md.

Questions: Risk Management (faculty and staff traveling without students) or the Glavin Office of International Education (students, and faculty and staff traveling with students).

Travel Abroad and Return Policy: https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/risk-management/Travel-Abroad-and-Return-Policy-September-2022.pdf

## Domestic travel during a crisis

Babson prohibits domestic employee travel to locations directly affected by a health, safety, or security incident or crisis as determined by the College, except for pre-approved essential travel.

- Policy: https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/risk-management/Domestic-Travel-Policy-During-a-Health-Safety-or-Security-Incident-or-Crisis.pdf

## Rail

Use the lowest available fare for reserved seating. First class is allowed for scheduled travel over seven hours.

## Local transportation and Logan Airport

- Public transportation is encouraged. Free hotel shuttles, discount shuttles, and shared cabs are preferred over a single-person cab.
- Babson generally reimburses up to $150 per one-way trip, with receipts, for any means of transportation to and from Logan Airport. Town cars should not be used.
- Uber is an option. Private sedans are not reimbursable unless they don't exceed the comparable taxi fare or are required for joint travel with senior leadership.
- Preferred services: Boston Coach, 800-672-7676, account 9081. Charles River Transportation, 508-651-1112. Wellesley Carriage, 508-653-5027 or 800-836-0006.

Bus charters: for 8 or more passengers, all bus or minivan users must book with Academy Bus, LLC (http://www.academybus.com) under an exclusivity agreement. The College won't support or pay for other providers.

## Personal cars and mileage

Mileage is reimbursed at the IRS rate (https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/standard-mileage-rates), which covers gas, wear, and insurance. Parking and tolls are reimbursable with receipts. Citations and repairs are not.

On the expense report, provide trip purpose, miles driven, date and destination, toll and parking receipts, and a Google Maps or MapQuest map showing the start and destination.

Your personal auto insurance is primary for liability and is the only coverage for damage to your own car. College insurance provides excess liability, and Workers' Compensation applies if employees are injured.

## Rental cars

- Use a P-Card. Rent up to midsize/intermediate, or a minivan with three or more people on College business.
- Larger classes are allowed if authorized ones are unavailable, the upgrade is free, extra baggage space is needed, or for pre-approved medical reasons.
- Enterprise and National are preferred under the E&I contract, using corporate account XZ79213. The contract runs 01/01/2020 to 12/31/2026. Book at https://elink.enterprise.com/en/22/12/babson-college.html Contract number CNR01494.
- Sign as: NAME OF TRAVELER, TITLE, BABSON COLLEGE.
- **In the U.S.:** decline collision damage insurance (CDW or LDW), personal accident insurance, and liability supplements. These are covered through the P-Card and College insurance.
- **Abroad:** accept all insurance coverage, including liability and physical damage.
- Repairs, parking tickets, traffic fines, and rental club fees aren't reimbursable.

Drivers must be authorized. See driver-and-vehicle-use.md. The corporate account can also be used for personal travel: https://www.nationalcar.com/enroll/XZ79213

## Lodging

Daily spending guidelines, including taxes:

| Location | Per day |
|---|---|
| Most U.S. cities | $240-$280 |
| Boston, San Francisco, Miami | $370-$400 |
| New York City | $385-$420 |

Higher costs need Workday approval from the authorized Vice President, Division Director, Vice Provost, or Division Chair.

- A single room with private bath is allowed. Suite or executive floor upgrades only at no additional cost, documented in Workday, or with prior approval if required for business.
- At a conference, a host hotel is fine even if not the most economical, when it's the most convenient or offers a discount rate.
- Hotel staff tips are reimbursable within reason, usually 15%.
- Not reimbursed: room service beyond normal meal expenses, in-room movies, in-room alcohol, and recreation other than basic fitness center fees.
- Staying with friends or family: a thank-you gift of no more than $75 per stay, with a receipt.
- Cancel with the hotel or agency and record the cancellation number and name. No-show charges generally aren't reimbursed.

## Meals

For full-day or overnight trips, three meals including beverage and tip are reimbursed up to $90 per day. Amounts can be pooled per day, but a separate receipt is required for each meal.

| Meal | Maximum | When |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | $15 | Departure from campus or residence before 7 a.m. |
| Lunch | $25 | Day trip lunches are not reimbursable |
| Dinner | $50 | Return to campus or residence after 7 p.m. |

At conferences that include breakfast and lunch, spend no more than $50 for dinner. Tips are normally 15-20%.

Business meals (with donors or colleagues, with specific business discussions) are reimbursed at reasonable actual cost as determined by the authorized signer. The IRS requires guest names, titles or affiliations, and the business purpose.

Meals with other employees are reimbursed only when confidentiality requires meeting off premises, at an out-of-town conference, or when authorized by the department head for reward, recognition, or other appropriate purposes.

For alcohol rules, see alcohol-and-catering.md.

## Expense reports

- File no later than three weeks after completing a trip, with documentation attached in Workday. P-Card transactions have their own 30-day rule. See p-card.md.
- Accounts Payable reimburses within two weeks when reports are on time, approved, and documented.
- If actual expenses exceed estimates by more than 10%, the appropriate Vice President, Division Director, Vice Provost, or Division Chair must approve.
- Meal and entertainment reimbursements need a receipt for every transaction and a list of everyone present.

## Other rules

- Business calls on a personal mobile device are reimbursable if you have no College-issued device. Attach the bill and reason in Workday.
- Entertainment is reimbursed for events with a business discussion during, immediately before, or immediately after.
- Laundry and dry cleaning are reimbursable for inclement weather or trips over five consecutive days.
- Return unused tickets to the agency for credit.
- Travel insurance: group travel insurance covers employees worldwide on College business. Extra flight insurance isn't reimbursable.
- Spouse expenses aren't reimbursed. Traveling with minors other than enrolled students is strongly discouraged and needs prior authorization from the Dean of the College.
- Costs before the start or after the end of business travel, such as vacation extensions, aren't reimbursable and shouldn't go on a P-Card.
- Government-grant-funded travelers must also follow the funding authority's requirements.

## Where the Business Travel web page differs

If someone asks about these, give the Guidelines answer and mention the page says otherwise, and suggest confirming with Procurement:

- Agencies: the page lists Great International Travel (617-559-3735) instead of Protravel International.
- Rail: the page allows first class over 6 hours, not 7.
- International business class: the page allows it for Alaska, Hawaii, and any destination outside North America, without the seven-hour condition.
- Tips: the page says 10-20% instead of 15-20%.
- Charles River Transportation: the page lists 1-800-444-1511.
- Rental liability: the page says to buy liability coverage up to $250,000 if not in the rate, unless renting on a P-Card.
- Lodging gift: the page says a gift to a host "should be $75".
