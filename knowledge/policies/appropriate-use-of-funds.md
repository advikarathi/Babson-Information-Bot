# Policy on Appropriate Use of Funds

Source: https://www.babson.edu/business-and-financial-services/procurement/policies-and-procedures/policy-on-appropriate-use-of-funds/
Last reviewed: 2026-11-05

This policy applies to all Babson employees and anyone authorized to spend Babson funds, and to all expenditures including P-Cards, advances, check requests, and purchase requisitions. Funds should be used for business purposes only.

For anything not listed: the expenditure must be appropriate, essential, and support the College's academic, institutional, or public service mission; it should not be personal in nature; and for employees it should not serve as additional compensation.

## Generally appropriate

- Meals or snacks for meetings and functions related to College business, reasonable in nature and occurrence. Use College dining services and facilities whenever possible.
- Meals for employees during emergencies or workload "crunch" times, using manager judgment.
- Alcohol only for approved campus events and business meals as outlined in the Travel Policy. Exceptions need prior approval from the appropriate Vice President, Dean, or Department Head.
- Annual holiday parties accessible to all department employees, approved by the Division or Department Head. Recommended limit: $25 or less per employee.
- Going-away receptions: reasonable low-cost food and non-alcoholic beverages, with Division or Department Head approval. Retirement and resignation gifts go through HR.
- Group birthday celebrations as part of a monthly departmental staff meeting: low-cost food, non-alcoholic beverages, and cards.

## Gifts

No more than one gift per division or department per event.

- Employee recognition: tangible items such as flowers, fruit or gift baskets, or Babson Campus Store items, approved by the Division or Department Head. Recommended limit: $75, to meet the IRS "de minimis" fringe benefit requirement. College-funded gift cards or certificates are not allowed.
- Student employee appreciation: small tangible gifts below $75 at the end of employment; low-cost food and non-alcoholic beverages with approval. Gift cards as thanks for student employee work are not acceptable.
- Flowers for condolences or hospitalization: recommended limit $100. Condolence flowers may be paid from the HR budget or a department's designated P-Card.

## Not appropriate

- Non-work personal events such as baby showers, wedding showers, and housewarmings, including gifts or gift cards for them.
- Alcohol other than as described above.

## Gift card exceptions

Gift cards may be used as thank-you gifts, prizes, or rewards only for non-employee guest speakers, student volunteers (for example tour guides), and non-employee students for campus-wide achievements, contests, or competitions and incentives for participation in student activities. See p-card.md for limits. Visa, MasterCard, or American Express gift cards redeemable for cash are not acceptable.

Other exceptions must be approved by the appropriate President's Cabinet member or designee.

## Consequences

Employees who spend College funds inappropriately, and managers who approve such expenditures, will reimburse the College and may face disciplinary action.
