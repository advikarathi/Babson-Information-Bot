## What it is

Babson provides qualified employees with a JP Morgan VISA Procurement Card to purchase goods that do not require a purchase order. P-Card use is strongly encouraged for small dollar purchases and for travel, meal, and entertainment expenses incurred on behalf of the College. It is for Babson business only. Personal purchases are strictly prohibited, and violations may result in revocation of privileges and possible disciplinary action.

Applying for and using the P-Card does not affect personal credit history. Babson assumes full corporate liability and pays JP Morgan directly.

Babson strongly suggests completing all business expenses with a P-Card rather than personal funds. Cardholders should only use a reimbursement request for mileage and extreme emergencies when a P-Card cannot be used.

## How to get a P-Card

Both full-time and part-time employees are eligible.

1. Log into Workday and click "Requests" on the home page.
2. Click "Create requests".
3. In the request type drop-down, click All and select "Procurement Card Application".
4. Answer the questions and click "Submit".

The employee and department head complete the application. Accounts Payable then orders the card from JP Morgan, and it is mailed to the employee's home. New cardholders get a message explaining how to complete the required policy sign-off in Workday.

Sign the card immediately and activate it by calling JP Morgan Customer Service at 1-800-316-6056. Only the person named on the card may use it.

Renewals: JP Morgan sends a new card to your home about two weeks before expiration. Sign and activate it on receipt.

## Spending limits

Each card has a single-transaction limit and a monthly limit. A purchase over either limit is declined at the point of sale.

| Profile | Monthly | Single transaction |
|---|---|---|
| Extra Light | $1,500 | $1,500 |
| Light | $5,000 | $2,500 |
| Medium | $15,000 | $10,000 |
| High | $25,000 | $15,000 |

The manual also states that the maximum per-transaction limit for any card is capped at $9,999, and that a purchase of $10,000 or more should be processed in Workday via a purchase order. Lead with that rule. If someone asks about the Medium or High single-transaction figures, point out that the manual lists both and suggest confirming with pcard@babson.edu.

To change a limit, temporarily for 30 days or permanently: in Workday, go to Requests and select "P-Card Limit Increase". It routes to your manager and then Accounts Payable for approval.

Before traveling, contact pcard@babson.edu to confirm your limits and whether your profile needs a temporary change.

## What you can buy

Use the P-Card for goods under $10,000, by telephone, mail, internet, or in person. Buy online from secure sites only and keep proof of purchase and receipt.

The P-Card should not be used to pay for services such as independent contractors, consultants, and awards. It should mainly be used for goods, though exceptions apply, for example memberships and subscriptions.

When ordering: use your home billing and shipping address. If a vendor asks for a PO number, use "P-CARD".

## Amazon

Since October 13, 2026, Amazon purchases for Babson go through the Amazon Business Punchout in Workday, not a P-Card. P-Cards may not be used on personal Amazon accounts, and those purchases will not be reimbursed. See amazon-punchout.md.

## Sales tax

As a 501(c)(3) non-profit, Babson does not pay Massachusetts sales tax. Babson's tax ID is 04-2103544 and Babson Global's is 27-1642647. Request the exemption at the point of purchase. If tax is charged anyway, the cardholder must make sure the vendor credits it back to the card. Accounts Payable can provide the Certificate of Exemption and Department of Revenue letter if a vendor needs them.

## Prohibited purchases

Examples from the manual:

- Alcoholic beverages, except as part of a restaurant meal or with approval of the appropriate division director and/or cost center manager
- Items redeemable for cash, including all gift cards (for example American Express, Visa, MasterCard)
- Gasoline for personal automotive use
- Printers, scanners, copiers, and fax machines (order these through Babson IT)
- Insurance
- Hazardous materials
- Animals
- Precious metals
- Travelers' checks
- Cash advances
- Firearms and ammunition
- Prescription drugs
- Personal items (the manual gives the example "Uber travel"). The Travel Guidelines, however, list Uber as an option for College business travel, so confirm with pcard@babson.edu if asked about business Uber trips.

## Gift cards

Babson, in line with IRS policy, does not allow P-Cards to buy gifts, gift cards, or gift certificates for employees, since these count as personal income. Gift cards to student employees as thanks for their work are not acceptable. Visa, MasterCard, and American Express gift cards are strictly prohibited.

Acceptable uses:
- Overseas alumni assisting locally at college fairs and similar events
- Subjects of research involving human subjects (not those conducting the research)
- Full-time students for campus-wide academic or co-curricular achievements, contests, or competitions
- Non-employees, not in lieu of pay. For example, a $50 gift card as a thank-you to an unpaid visiting speaker or an alumni event volunteer

Requirements:
- Describe how, why, and pertinent dates in the expense report memo field. Purchase within three months of distribution.
- Maximum $50 per participant without prior approval from the Department Cost Center Manager.
- The record must include recipient name and amount. Student name and ID must be provided with the expense report.
- If one participant receives $100 or more in a calendar year, obtain a completed W-9 and send it to Accounts Payable with the total value given.

Forms: Non-Employee Student Gift Card Report (https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/procurement/Non-Employee-Student-Gift-Card-Report-Secure.pdf) and Non-Employee Gift Card Report (https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/procurement/Non-Employee-Gift-Card-Report-Secure.pdf)

## Expensing, receipts, and deadlines

- Expense all transactions in Workday Expense Management within 30 days of each transaction.
- After that, an email reminder goes to the cardholder and their immediate supervisor.
- After 35 days of delinquent transactions, Accounts Payable may suspend the card until the transactions have cleared and the supervisor signs off on reinstatement.
- Assign each transaction the right expense item and cost center, and enter a detailed description in the Business Reason field.
- Receipts must be attached electronically for all transactions over $75. Documentation is encouraged for all amounts.
- For travel, meals, or entertainment, list participants and the business purpose regardless of amount.
- Approvers must log into Workday weekly to review expenses of their assigned cardholders.

Missing receipt: ask the vendor for a copy. If that fails, upload a Microsoft Word document describing the expense, labeled "Lost Receipt Affidavit".

Workday instructions: http://learnworkday.babson.edu (open the Financials icon, then Expense Reimbursement and P-Card Allocation).

## Lost or stolen card

Immediately notify JP Morgan at 1-800-270-7760 and Accounts Payable at pcard@babson.edu. Have your home ZIP code and the last 4 digits of your Workday ID ready. Confirm notification to pcard@babson.edu by the next business day.

## International travel

Tell JP Morgan your international travel plans and dates at 1-800-270-7760, with your home ZIP code and the last four digits of your Workday ID ready. For trouble while traveling, call the 24-hour number on the back of the card.

## Fraud and disputed charges

JP Morgan places a temporary hold and notifies the cardholder of possible fraud. If a charge is fraudulent, the card is cancelled and a new one issued within 3-5 business days.

The cardholder follows up on erroneous charges, returns, and adjustments with the vendor. If that fails, dispute the transaction through JP Morgan, which determines a resolution within 90 days.

## Accidental personal charges

Immediately reimburse the College: complete a Cash Sale in Workday, put a check payable to Babson College and a copy of the Cash Sale in a Cash Receipts Voucher Envelope, and submit it to Financial Services. Upload a copy of the check to the transaction with the receipt and enter "Used P-Card in error" in the narrative field.

## Suspension and cancellation

JP Morgan and Babson may suspend or cancel privileges at any time. On cancellation, the card must be shredded or destroyed. Misuse by anyone other than the named cardholder results in revocation and disciplinary action up to and including termination.
