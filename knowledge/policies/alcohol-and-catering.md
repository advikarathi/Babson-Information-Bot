The Alcohol and Catering Policy applies to internal and external groups, including student groups, hosting events at Babson locations other than the Babson Executive Conference Center. It does not apply to small, informal gatherings where attendees buy food and drink with personal funds.

## Buying alcohol: the rules at a glance

- **On-campus events:** all groups must use Chartwells for alcohol (except at the Babson Executive Conference Center). Chartwells is required to serve alcohol at all events. Groups cannot bring in their own alcohol.
- **What can be served:** in most cases, only beer and wine. The Events Management Office occasionally hosts events for staff, alumni, and friends (not students) where Chartwells serves hard alcohol.
- **Charging guests a fee:** for internal events on campus (excluding Roger's Pub and the Babson Executive Conference Center), any group charging guests a fee for any portion of the event must secure a one-day liquor license from the Town of Wellesley at least 45 days before the event, using the 12C Alcohol Permission Form: https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=3bdizUhLvUSQ5-FDoiyOrX-gIx6EueNKs5Le7qdmHzZUMVcyVjhGSENFVFNUSVNBNlY4NFRCMUpYRC4u&route=shorturl Contact catering@babson.edu for more information.
- **Alcohol with a cost to attendees, or at an event with a cost to attend:** no restrictions at the Pub. At any other location, email the Events Management Office (eventsmanagement@babson.edu) at least 60 days before the event. These two pages give different lead times (45 and 60 days), so confirm early with catering@babson.edu or eventsmanagement@babson.edu.
- **Alcohol at no cost to attendees:** use Babson Dining Services.
- **Donated alcohol:** all donations, including from corporations or distributors, must go through Chartwells' approved alcohol vendor. Contact Chartwells for details. For small donations for religious events, contact procurement@babson.edu or Risk Management.
- **Server training and security:** Chartwells provides TIPS server training for all on-campus events with alcohol. Public Safety decides whether officers are needed based on event size and scope.
- **Risk level:** serving, consuming, or selling alcohol is a risk-enhancing factor under the Risk Management Guide and can raise an event's risk rating.

### Paying for alcohol

- **College funds:** permitted only for approved campus events and business meals as outlined in the Travel Guidelines. Exceptions need prior approval from the appropriate Vice President, Dean, or Department Head.
- **P-Card:** alcohol is prohibited except as part of a restaurant meal or with approval of the appropriate division director and/or cost center manager.
- **Business travel:** a drink with dinner is reimbursable. Alcohol for business entertainment should be kept to a minimum, and in-room alcoholic beverages are not reimbursed.
- **Federal grants:** the Travel Guidelines say alcohol may not be charged to a federal grant. Link: https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/procurement/babson-college-travel-policy.pdf
- **Parties:** going-away receptions and student employee appreciation events are limited to non-alcoholic beverages when using College funds.

## Catering

Chartwells (Babson Dining Services) is the College's preferred caterer for all on-campus events. Contact them before booking or finalizing plans, at least seven business days before the event.

- Online: https://new.dineoncampus.com/babson/catering
- Email: catering@babson.edu
- Phone: the policy lists 781-239-4943 and the Third-Party Caterers page lists 781-239-4403

If Chartwells cannot meet your needs because of timeline, budget, or food requests, choose from the approved caterer list below. To use a caterer not on the list, contact Procurement to request vetting. A Certificate of Insurance is required as part of approval.

Vendors donating food or drink must meet the criteria for an approved third-party caterer. Contact procurement@babson.edu.

Caterers must have current catering permits from the Board of Health where they primarily operate and complete the Town of Wellesley catering form no less than five business days before the event: http://www.wellesleyma.gov/Pages/FOV1-0001FDB6/permits/CateringReg.pdf

## Which vendor can I use?

**Closed event** (Babson students, faculty, and staff only, with valid Babson One Cards; not family members, external partners, or Wellesley residents)
- Food or beverage under $500 on a Purchase Card: no restrictions
- Over $500 on a Purchase Card or College Check: Babson Dining Services or an approved caterer

**Open event** (no attendance restrictions)
- All food or beverage on a Purchase Card or College Check: Babson Dining Services, or an approved caterer with a Service Provider Agreement managed through Procurement

**Private meeting** (a small specific group, such as a club's membership, an office party, or a committee retreat)
- Under $500 on a Purchase Card: no restrictions
- Under $500 on a College Check: a Service Provider Agreement with proof of insurance, managed through Procurement

## Food trucks

Must be sponsored by a College department, complete the College Vendor Agreement, provide proof of insurance, and then contact the Town of Wellesley to submit paperwork and schedule a Board of Health inspection. Student organizations and departments must meet with Events Management (eventsmanagement@babson.edu), Student Activities and Leadership (sal@babson.edu), or Graduate Student Affairs (gradsa@babson.edu) at least 60 days before the event.

## Fundraisers selling food or beverages

Must be managed with Babson Dining Services. The only exception is pre-packaged, individually wrapped commercial products not needing temperature control. Contact Events Management, Student Activities and Leadership, or Graduate Student Affairs at least 30 days before. Products must be stored securely on a shelf or palette, not in a box on the floor, and only Babson Dining staff or the distributor may transport them onto campus.

## Student food or beverage businesses

Must comply with Town of Wellesley Board of Health regulations. Academic ones must consult the managing department. Personal ones need support from the Blank Center (blankcenter@babson.edu) or the Institute for Social Innovation.

Students, faculty, and staff may not contact the Wellesley Board of Health without explicit permission. Email foodcompliance@babson.edu for support.
