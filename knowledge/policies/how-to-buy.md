## About the Procurement office

The Procurement office provides professional attention to each department's needs and develops systems, policies, and procedures that ensure the best value for the college. Its responsibilities include contract management, dispute management and resolution, consortia relationships, training on proper procurement practices, and financial analysis.

IT software requests, IT software renewals, and IT project requests use this form: https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/35e5c20ad3814c63b54b2975e37ddf4f

## Plan ahead

Forecast departmental needs as far in advance as possible. Some vendors need four weeks or more to process an order. "ASAP" orders increase costs and administrative workload.

## Rules to remember

- Payment for goods and services should be made after goods are received and services are rendered.
- Most services (with exceptions) require a contract, submitted through Workday for approval before services are rendered.
- All goods and services should be entered into Workday before being rendered or delivered, once the supplier is confirmed in Workday by Accounts Payable.
- A purchase order, procurement card, or check request are the only authorized methods of purchasing goods or services for the College.
- Do not commit College funds yourself by placing an order or signing a contract without following procurement procedures. You could be held legally liable and responsible for payment of unauthorized purchases.
- An "after-the-fact" purchase order cannot be issued after placing an order with a vendor.

## When you need a purchase order (PO)

- PO numbers are required for all requests over $10,000. Procurement creates PO numbers from supplier contracts or requisitions submitted into Workday.
- Some requests over $10,000 may not require a PO. Follow up with Procurement for more information.
- All services totaling $10,000 or more cumulatively for the year require a purchase order.
- Purchase orders are not required for purchases under $10,000. For those, P-Card use is very strongly encouraged.

Why POs matter: they support budget management, create a budgetary commitment that helps prevent an unexpected shortage of funds at fiscal year end, and are the only way Procurement, Receiving, Mail Services, and Accounts Payable can track an order.

After a requisition is approved, Procurement issues the PO to the selected vendor and emails the PO number to the requestor.

## Requisitions and supplier contracts in Workday

- Requisition for Goods: when requesting goods over $10,000.
- Requisition for Services: when procuring services and an agreement is not required, mostly orders under $10,000 each year. Exceptions may apply.
- Supplier Contract: when procuring services and an agreement is required, mostly orders over $10,000 each year. Exceptions may apply.

## Competitive bidding

- All transactions of $10,000 and greater must be competitively bid.
- For orders under $100,000, attach three competitive quotes to your submission in Workday.
- For orders over $100,000, Procurement conducts a formal bid process or Request for Proposal (RFP). Contact procurement@babson.edu to start.
- Splitting a transaction into smaller amounts, delaying or staggering purchases, or using multiple staff members to buy the same or related items to avoid bidding are serious policy violations, reported to senior management as unauthorized purchases. Responsible employees may face disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- Procurement negotiates final proposals and contracts. The department, as the owner of the relationship, makes the final determination of who receives the award.
- Suppliers must contact Procurement to obtain bid results. Babson does not reveal specific pricing or other information associated with bid processes. Vendors should propose their most competitive offer on first submittal, since these are considered best and final offers.

## If three bids cannot be obtained (no-bid or sole source exception)

Complete the Purchase Order Bid Process Exception Form and submit it with a requisition in Workday for approval. Completing the form does not guarantee approval. Goods or services available from more than one supplier remain subject to competitive bidding. Sole source cases need backup documentation.

Form: https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/procurement/no-bid-exception-form-(1).docx

## How Procurement selects vendors

Procurement considers quality, cost, delivery, terms, and past vendor performance. Vendors must show they can supply on a regular and timely basis under varying market conditions and procure adequate raw materials, should be cost competitive, and should meet specifications and industry standards.

Additional consideration may go to vendors developing new or exclusive products, with adequate financial strength and a reputation for meeting specifications and schedules, located near the College, that have helped advance the College's educational goals, or that support the College's Affirmative Action Policy.

## Delivery and problems with an order

- All shipments of goods must be received through Central Receiving. Ship-to address: Babson College, ATTN: (your name or department / the purchase order number), Central Services, 231 Forest Street, Babson Park, MA 02457-0310.
- Orders are generally delivered to your department within 24 hours of receipt, provided purchasing procedures are followed.
- Your department verifies quantity and quality on delivery. If an order is incorrect or you are returning items, immediately inform Procurement.
