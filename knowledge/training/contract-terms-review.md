# Training: Contracts at Babson

Source: Contract Terms Review Process presentation: https://www.babson.edu/media/babson/assets/business-and-financial-services/Contract-Process.pdf
Last reviewed: 2026-11-05

Babson uses a risk-based approach to contracts: who should, can, and will bear the risk of loss.

## Completing a template

Templates are fillable PDFs with instructions. Master agreements are available for the Professional Services and Service Provider agreements on request.

- Babson College is always the contracting party.
- Type the legal name of the counterparty in both places.
- State the services or work to be performed.
- State the maximum amount to be paid.
- Don't sign the contract until reviews are complete.
- Make sure the business terms are clear.

## Common pitfalls

- Not using a College template
- Using the wrong template
- Not completing the template correctly
- Not including the previous agreement
- No signed contract in place before work begins
- A counterparty can't be paid without a signed contract

## Key terms to watch

| Term | Babson's position |
|---|---|
| Warranties | "This is their stuff to sell or license to us" |
| Indemnity | The College should not pay for damage caused by the other party |
| Privacy | No unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of data or information |
| Intellectual property | Work paid for by the College entirely belongs to the College |
| Limits of liability | The other party must stand behind its work |
| Disputes | Trials, in Massachusetts under Massachusetts law |
| Termination | Every contract needs an exit strategy |
| Amendments | In writing and signed by both, not oral or automatic |
| Use of Babson's name | Rarely permitted, with prior written approval required |
