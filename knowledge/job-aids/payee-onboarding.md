A payee is any non-employee individual or organization receiving a payment from Babson, including suppliers, service providers, and students or individuals receiving gifts, prizes, awards, stipends, or other non-payroll payments. A payee must complete onboarding, including payment and tax verification, before payment can be processed.

## Babson employees requesting a new payee

Submit the request in Workday Strategic Sourcing (WSS), available from the Workday menu. If it isn't there, add it through Add Apps by searching for Strategic Sourcing. In WSS, go to Navigate, then Payee Onboarding. Full steps are in the Requester Job Aid linked above.

For questions, use Team Chat within your request or contact Accounts Payable.

## Suppliers and payees completing onboarding

1. You receive an email from Babson with a link to the form in the WSS portal.
2. Verify your email with a one-time code.
3. Create your account with your name and a password, and set up multi-factor authentication by SMS or authenticator app.
4. On the home screen, click Manage, then Edit to open the form.
5. Complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk:
   - Supplier information: legal name, category, and group. Non-employee students receiving a gift, prize, or award select "Non-Employee Student/Award".
   - Business information: address, phone, and tax information.
   - Banking information: for electronic payment.
   - Contact information: the person responsible for the form.
   - Attachments: signed and dated tax forms and other required documents.
6. Click Submit, or Save as Draft to return later.

**How you get paid:** with a bank account on file, you're paid by ACH or electronic payment. Without one, you're paid by check. Babson doesn't send checks to international addresses.

**International payees** also get an email from noreply@sprintax.com with a tax questionnaire. Complete it promptly to avoid payment delays.

**After you submit:** the status shows Approval Pending while Accounts Payable reviews it. Information may be validated through third-party verification services. If corrections are needed, the form may be returned to you to edit and resubmit. Once approved, payments may be processed.

**Questions:** use the Message Center in the WSS portal or contact Babson's Accounts Payable team.

Tax forms are listed in accounts-payable.md.
