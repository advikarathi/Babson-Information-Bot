## Purchasing goods

Goods are tangible items, with some exceptions.

1. Under $10,000: a Babson P-Card can be used. Over $10,000: submit a requisition in Workday.
2. Purchases should be competitively bid, so shop around when permissible. Attach three competitive quotes from different suppliers or a no-bid exception form.
3. Submit the Request Non-Catalog Items task in Workday and select Request Goods.
4. Once approved, Procurement generates a Purchase Order for orders over $10,000.
5. Once goods are received, submit the Create Supplier Invoice Request task, referencing your PO number. It routes to Accounts Payable.

## Purchasing services

Services require contracts and should be submitted through Workday for approval before services are rendered.

1. Choose and complete a Babson contract template. See contracts.md. Use the department name, not a faculty name, for "on behalf of", and fill in all blank fields including contact information.
2. Route the contract:
   - **Unaltered Babson template:** route for signature from both parties and attach a copy in Workday.
   - **Supplier changed the template:** submit through the Contract Submittal Form for Legal review (https://babson.tfaforms.net/102) and wait for an email from Legal before signing.
   - **Supplier's own contract:** this also needs Legal review. Submit the documents unsigned by both parties through the same form.
3. Once the supplier returns the signed contract, send it to your department's designated signer.
4. Submit the fully executed contract and supporting documents, such as software approval, Independent Contractor form, or Certificate of Insurance, using the Create Supplier Contract task in Workday. To request a COI waiver, note it in the Memo section.
5. Once approved, Procurement generates a Purchase Order for orders over $10,000.
6. Set up payment using the Create Supplier Invoice Request task. It routes to Accounts Payable.

Large purchases such as major software use the Software and IT Project Request form: https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/35e5c20ad3814c63b54b2975e37ddf4f
